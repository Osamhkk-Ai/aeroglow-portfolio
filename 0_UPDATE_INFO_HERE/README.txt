HOW TO UPDATE YOUR INFO:

1. TO UPDATE YOUR SOCIAL LINKS AND TEXT:
Open the `portfolio_and_socials.ts` file in this folder. You can easily edit your name, tagline, GitHub link, LinkedIn link, and email here. The whole site will automatically update.

2. TO UPDATE YOUR RESUME (CV):
Just drop your new PDF file into this folder and delete the old one. Make sure it is the ONLY PDF file in this folder! The website will automatically use it for the "Resume" and "Download CV" buttons.

Note: You don't need to name your PDF anything specific, the site just finds the PDF in this folder automatically.
